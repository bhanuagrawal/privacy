Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

PDF Splitter – Offline (also referred to as "PdfKit" or "PDF Kit"; the "App") is developed and operated by DabblePoint (developer name shown on the Google Play Store listing; account owner: Bhanu Agrawal, bhanuagrawal001@gmail.com).

1. Information the App Collects

The App processes PDF documents locally on your device.

  • Personal and document data: The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information or the contents of your documents. All PDF processing (splitting, merging, compressing, converting, rearranging) is performed locally on your device. We never upload your files to any server.
  • Storage access: The App requests access to your device's storage solely to read and save PDF files you choose. These files remain on your device and are never transmitted to us.
  • Usage analytics (automatic diagnostics): The App uses the Google Firebase Analytics SDK, which automatically sends certain diagnostic and usage data to Google on our behalf, such as: app open/foreground/background events, screen views, app readily understandable heartbeat events including app uninstall events (app_remove), app version, device model, operating system version, and language. We do not use advertising identifiers and do not link this data to your identity. For details see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Crash reporting: Crash diagnostics may be collected via Google Play/Firebase services to improve stability.

2. Data Integrity

Your PDF files remain on your device at all times. We have no access to their contents.

3. Third-Party Services

The App is distributed through the Google Play Store (service provider: Google LLC) and integrates Firebase Analytics (service provider: Google LLC) for automatic usage and stability analytics as described above. Analytics data is processed by Google according to Google's own privacy policies.

4. Children

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
